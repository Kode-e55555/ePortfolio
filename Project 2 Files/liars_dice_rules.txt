Liar's Dice Rules

1. Liar's Dice is a multiplayer game with a minimum of two players.
2. Each player has five dice. All dice are rolled once at the start of the game.
3. On a turn, a player makes a guess about how many dice of a certain number exist among all players.
   Example: 'two fives' means the player believes there are at least two dice showing five.
4. Bluffing is allowed. Players can call 'liar' if they think the last guess is incorrect.
5. If 'liar' is called, all dice are revealed:
   - If the guess was correct, the guesser wins.
   - If the guess was wrong, the person who called 'liar' wins.
6. If the guess is not challenged, the next player must increase the bid:
   - The quantity must be higher than the previous guess.
   - Examples: from 'two fives' to 'three fives' or 'three fours'.
   - Invalid follow-ups: 'one five' or 'two ones'.
7. Gameplay continues until a 'liar' is called.
